You can grant your colleagues access to the <Glossary>EVA</Glossary> portal and your plans by adding them to your team.

To add new members to your team:

1. Navigate to the [EVA portal](https://api.kyc.moodys.com/eva/portal/login) and log in with SSO.
2. Select the **Manage account** icon and choose the **Team** option from the menu.

   <Image align="center" width="300px" src="https://files.readme.io/d2616195eaafdc5df4e9a048bb16522e278c3e36d1a1e71453d922afc957ffaf-EVA_portal_manage_account_menu.png" />
3. Select **Invite new members**.
4. Enter the email addresses of the colleagues you would like to invite to access the EVA portal and select **Send invitation**.

An email is sent to each email address inviting the user to join your team on the EVA portal. Each user should follow these steps to complete their invitation:

1. Open the invitation email and select **Join the team**.
2. Select **Log in with SSO**.

   <Image align="center" width="300px" src="https://files.readme.io/06b911bd6facca80f75bf767967db2bd5f2ac19fa9f665077dadedfdc03cff4f-EVA_portal_invitation_log_in_screen.png" />
3. Select **Accept invite** to log in to the EVA portal.
