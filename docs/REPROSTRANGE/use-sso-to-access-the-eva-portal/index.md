You can use single-sign on (SSO) to access the EVA portal.

To set up SSO access for the portal:

1. Navigate to the EVA <Anchor label="portal" target="_blank" href="https://api.kyc.moodys.com/eva/portal/login">portal</Anchor>.
2. Select **Log in with SSO**.
3. From the Moody's email screen that opens, select the **Sign up** link.

   <Image align="center" border={true} width="350px" src="https://files.readme.io/eebf93a796e02884088c0b5aa5c5c3981217657c2125f2821e37a8ab0ec1e61f-EVA_Moodys_login_screen.png" className="border" />
4. Add your details into the form that opens and select **Register**.

   An email is sent to you to verify the SSO account.
5. From the email, select the **Activate your Account** option.
6. Return to the EVA portal and select **Log in with SSO** again.
7. Enter the email address you used to register. Then, select **Next**.
8. Enter your password and select **Sign in**.
9. Accept the General Terms & Conditions to continue and log in to the EVA portal.

If you have activated your account but experience an error when logging in, wait a few minutes and log in again. If the issue doesn't resolve itself, contact our support team at [clientservices@moodys.com](mailto:clientservices@moodys.com).
