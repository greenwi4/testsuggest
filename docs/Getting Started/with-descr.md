## Introduction

To make sure our brand is recognised, understood and loved, we need to develop a shared understanding of who we are, why we exist and how we carry ourselves in the world.

To help us achieve this, EPI developed the **Wero brand portal**. The site offers a comprehensive and accessible overview of the principles and rules to ensure a consistent representation of Wero in digital and physical spaces.

## Audience

- **Marketers and creative partners** of EPI and of EPI scheme members, who develop and deploy marketing communications about Wero in the Wero communication channels and in the scheme members’ channels.
- **Designers and copywriters** of EPI and of EPI scheme members, who design the Wero user experiences across the Wero use cases or implement Wero brand assets in mobile banking apps.
- **EPI employees** who send communications about Wero internally or externally.

## Content overview

The Wero brand portal is a continuously evolving and collaborative tool that features practical guidance and assets for bringing the Wero brand to life in every medium.

The portal includes the following sections:

### Brand foundations

The objectives, positioning and personality of the Wero brand. These foundations equip creators of Wero experiences with a common definition of the brand’s purpose and behaviour so that the brand is both distinctive and consistent.

### Visual identity

Design rules and source files for all visual elements—such as logos, colours, typefaces and co-branding lockups—which, together, uniquely represent Wero and are supported by concrete application examples.

### Tone of voice

Principles, tips and tricks that ensure the Wero brand speaks in a consistent way in every context and for all audiences.

### Marketing guidelines for EPI scheme members

The messaging, branding and creative guidance for EPI scheme members who publish information about Wero in their own communication channels.

### Wero campaign guidelines

An overview of the marketing strategy for the Wero launch along with creative specifications, for EPI scheme members to amplify the launch in their own communication channels.
